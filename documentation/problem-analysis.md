# Problem Analysis

Recruitment teams often rely on separate spreadsheets, inboxes, and meeting tools. This causes duplicate candidate records, unclear application status, and weak accountability. TalentSync centralizes job posting, resume-backed applications, candidate tracking, interviews, and offers while preserving separate service and database ownership.

Users are Candidates and Recruiters. Candidates browse jobs, apply, and track progress. Recruiters create their own jobs, review candidates, schedule interviews, and manage offers.
